# LICENSE & GAME DISTRIBUTION COMPLIANCE POLICY

## 1. Principles of Legal Game Distribution
PlayGrid adheres strictly to legal, authorized distribution standards:
- **No Unauthorized Scraping:** PlayGrid strictly forbids automated scraping, unauthorized ripping, or illicit hosting of games from third-party flash/HTML5 portals.
- **Verification of Rights:** Every game title in the PlayGrid catalog is either:
  1. An original proprietary game developed by PlayGrid Studios (e.g., Pixel Runner, Space Blaster 3000, Snake Retro X).
  2. A verified commercial license granted directly by the game developer or indie studio.
  3. Distributed under a recognized permissive open-source license (MIT, Apache 2.0, Creative Commons) with explicit developer attribution.

## 2. Ingestion & Approval Procedure
Before any game title is published to the network catalogue:
1. The developer or licensing manager submits a zip package along with licensing terms to the Super Admin Ingestion Queue.
2. The super-admin inspects external asset calls to ensure no unauthorized tracking or third-party ad injection occurs.
3. The game is assigned a unique `game_id`, verified origin tags, and sandbox permission parameters.

## 3. DMCA & Intellectual Property Takedown
Rights holders may report suspected copyright infringement via `/contact` or directly to `legal@playgrid.io`. Inquiries appear immediately in the Super Admin Moderation Desk (`/admin/moderation`), and reported titles are suspended pending review.
